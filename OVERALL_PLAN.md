## Owner-approved v13: parameterized Decisions-style route optimization, not first-legal simulations (2026-11-10)

**One optimizer, tunable resolution.** A **single high-level invocation** accepts `start_level`, `end_level`, `level_stride` (or explicit sampled levels), `mob_count`, compute/search budget and scenario/ruleset. A first wide/sparse configuration is `start_level=20, end_level=60, level_stride=10, mob_count=3`: optimize L20, L30, L40, L50, L60 **together in one optimizer request**. The dense/full configuration uses the **same optimizer**, `start_level=10, end_level=60, level_stride=1, mob_count=100`. Stride and target count are *parameters*, not separate workflows, modes, milestones, special-case algorithms, or M1 gates. One high-level optimizer call may orchestrate many pinned Rust/native evaluation calls internally; it does not mean one Rust combat invocation for every level/build.

**Normalize the small-mob objective by fully paid terminal recovery.** Every candidate's `mob_count` native hostile pulls are followed by genuine source-native **recovery to full HP *and* full mana**, charging **all** simulated time and consumables. Optimize `combat + movement/pulls + survival + terminal recovery-to-full`, not raw DPS, kill time or a free reset. Let the optimizer choose legal Life Tap (HP→mana), eating, drinking and passive regeneration according to the available actor/spells/consumables and actual engine rules; iteratively account for both resources (Life Tap may improve mana while reducing HP). **No hypothetical instant full refill or fixed free aftercare.** If native behavior cannot complete or observe restoration, report the precise inability and an unscored/provisional candidate; do not mark it comparable. This paid full-recovery boundary intentionally reduces short-sample resource-debt bias and makes 3-mob screens more comparable to longer 100-mob samples; it is still an estimator, not proof of equal rankings, and it must not invent a full-HP/mana transition in the actual continuous route.

**Optimize across choices and levels, not just simulate legality.** On each selected level, search multiple Rust-valid Affliction/Demonology/Destruction/hybrid talents and continuing talent paths, equipment, pets, APL/DoT/pull policies and recovery choices. The backend's deterministic `balanced` mode uses **one iteration/repeat per candidate by default** for search; additional balanced repetitions are not independent stochastic confidence samples. Within the compute budget, keep competing legal cross-level frontiers and rank candidates on charged native elapsed time plus legal level progression and costs. Sparse-level winners are provisional guideposts, not automatically contiguous legal 10–60 talent paths. As budget allows, increase level density, mob count, candidate breadth and finalist validation **without changing optimizer architecture**. Longer or stochastic checks are decision-driven (or independently required by unchanged M1 contracts), not serial default 100-mob payments for the first native-preparable build.

**Evidence and compute:** A first-preparable talent addition, greedy full-tree fill or single native 100-mob run is a **baseline/feasibility observation**, not an optimized build. Preserve and reuse original genuine paid L20–L60 baseline receipts and the bounded greedy L47 family comparison; never re-pay them solely for more coverage. Reports identify evaluated levels, actual build/path alternatives and dimensions, `mob_count`, source/binary, `balanced` iteration count, terminal HP/mana, paid recovery time, scorer, elapsed score, baseline delta and missing mechanics/stat weights. Say "best of tested candidates" unless stronger evidence exists. This decision does **not** change any of the nine M1 gates, invalidate permanent recorded PASSes, or create an extra acceptance requirement.

## Owner-approved v7 single-source architecture amendment (2026-11-04)

**MythicSim Rust is the source of game facts and available optimization choices.** Its pinned, maintained fork owns items/equipment and enchants, spells and ranks, class talent trees/rank limits and eligibility, available APL actions/conditions, full-build legality and Warcraft mechanics. These data and relevant preparation/spellbook information already exist in Rust; reuse them rather than copy them into Forever. Their existence does **not** imply that an exhaustive legal-options enumeration API is already implemented or that all restrictions are verified: implement any *actually needed* missing option/capability or legality in the Rust fork, test it there and promote a tested exact SHA.

**Forever owns the scenario and generic optimizer only:** it requests native available alternatives for the class/level/spec/scenario, proposes and recombines gear × enchants × talents × APL candidates, asks the selected pinned native engine to prepare/validate/execute whole builds, and scores/searches actual results. No permanent Python GameIR, copied SQLite item source, class-specific Python talent eligibility or duplicate Warcraft game-rule catalogue. Retired P1/P2-only code is deleted, not rebuilt as a new Rust/Python proof pipeline. Migrate *live* P3–P7.5/optimizer consumers without breaking them before deleting transitional dependencies. The currently approved Go execution alternative is not silently outlawed by the Rust-owned target architecture.

**v7 governs game-fact ownership, not the current acceptance costs.** v8's active acceptance is defined in the current PROCESS and gate contracts; no new gate or duplicate MythicSim validation is introduced.

## Product goal

Build a generic, source-driven WoW Forever 1–60 solo leveling simulator/optimizer for all nine classes. Given class, level, legal constraints and a pinned Forever ruleset, automatically discover and explain the best **known executable** combination of talents, equipment, combat/rotation policy, pull strategy and paid recovery/survival. Optimize comparable **real elapsed time per mob/route**, not only DPS, arbitrary XP proxies, or a preset guide build.

A user must be able to inspect actual selected gear with item identities and scored alternatives; talent ranks and next talent; the actual legal actions/rotations and their measured score; stat weights; encounter, survival and recovery; and why the chosen configuration beat benchmark alternatives. The engine must be generic; game facts and the optimizer's available alternatives come from the exact-SHA-pinned MythicSim Rust source of truth, not class-specific Python catalogues or hardcoded winner builds.

## M0.3 — Direct native Rust API (owner-approved 2026-11-11)

**Outcome:** Forever consumes the pinned MythicSim Rust game options, whole-build preparation and native real-world simulation/recovery through a direct typed callable interface or persistent request/response session. Do not create source/prepared JSON intermediate files per candidate, execute a new CLI process for every candidate, or add parallel Python Warcraft legality implementations. JSON remains acceptable as a wire format and for final saved results, not as file-based orchestration.

**Scope:** Class/level/ruleset/scenario-parametric native `get_options`, `prepare` and `evaluate` must expose usable gear, enchant, talent, spell and APL alternatives, accepted/refused whole builds, gsim↔Rust spatial-world ACK execution and genuinely paid terminal recovery to full health **and** mana. Native Forever options exclude items with combat-rating stats (not a Forever stat), and retain native percent stats as percent, with no rating conversion. One rejected item drops that candidate, not the entire search. Verify real native results and errors through a focused integration run; do not compare Rust's deterministic-protobuf SHA against JSON-file SHA or create extra result-acceptance gates.

**Boundary:** M0.3 is the reusable engine API, **not** the first successful optimized multi-level outcome. That is M0.4. Source-native game rules and option legality stay in Rust for every class; no Warlock-only API or hardcoded winner.

## M0.4 — First genuine successful generic optimize invocation (owner-approved 2026-11-11)

**Exact required one-command delivery:**

```bash
python -m src.v2.runner optimize \
  --class warlock \
  --tree unconstrained \
  --start-level 20 \
  --end-level 60 \
  --level-stride 20 \
  --n-mobs 2 \
  --recover-at-end true \
  --rng balanced \
  --backend rust
```

**Completion means this command runs successfully using the M0.3 API and real pinned Rust + gsim/native execution**, without manually launching subruns, supplying known winners, inserting a Warlock-specific orchestration script or switching to a mocked evaluator. Within the same optimizer request it searches actually competing talent families/hybrids, equipment, pet and APL/pull/recovery policies at **L20, L40 and L60**, including cross-level/backward seeding, and produces **at least one complete mutually compatible measured sampled-level build path**. Preserve slower early candidates when later-level cumulative performance can make their paths better. The reported output includes per-level talents, actual gear item identities, pet, APL, scored alternative builds, genuine two-mob combat/movement elapsed time, real terminal recovery to **100% HP and 100% mana**, and authentic engine/scenario receipts. Neither a first-legal build nor three independent per-level winners is optimization.

**Level stride is sampling, not a demand to simulate skipped levels.** Do not run or require combat or preparation at L21–39 or L41–59 for this request. Sampled-compatible allocations must follow source-native legal point investment with no invented respec; unmeasured intermediate progression, XP, travel and training must be labeled unmeasured, not fabricated as a continuous 20–60 leveling run. The same generic optimizer supports other classes, strides, mob counts and budgets as parameters, not distinct algorithms.

**No false-green outcome:** unit tests, mock results, green CI without genuine native combat, exit code zero with no winner, an empty `NO_COMPLETE_COMPATIBLE_SAMPLED_PATH` result, unranked/refused candidates or an SHA-identity paperwork check do **not** satisfy M0.4. Reuse already-paid compatible observations; retain precise native error reasons and continue over other valid item/build candidates. M0.4 delivers a real sparse result; M0.5 expands it to a playable continuous Warlock 10–60 route, while M0.6 handles fully autonomous end-to-end execution. No new M1 gate or change to permanent accepted PASSes.

No per-level workflows, Warlock-only evaluator, handcrafted JSON route, mandatory skipped-level prepare or new tests/certificates for changing stride.

**Delivery steps, in order within the same optimizer:** after M0.4's demonstrated L20/L40/L60 result at stride=20, execute a real `optimize` at stride=10 (L20/30/40/50/60), then stride=5 (L20/25/30/35/40/45/50/55/60), then stride=1 (all L20–60 levels). Each step must return at least one **genuinely source-native measured, jointly compatible ranked path** at every requested sampled level, with selected talent allocations, item identities, pet, APL/pull/recovery policy, competing native observations, true elapsed time including two-mob final restoration to both full HP and full mana, plus honest refusals/limitations. Reuse identical paid candidate/scenario observations across densification and cross-seed previous promising complete paths instead of restarting search. Iterate budget and frontier breadth to obtain real competition, not first-legal baselines.

**Definition of success:** same production entry point, no new special mode, and actual measured compatible paths through all four requested strides. A green test, an empty path, independent per-level winners or a claimed continuous XP/travel/dual-spec replay is not M0.5 completion. At stride=1 the search contains all L20–60 sampled levels but this by itself is **not** proof of continuous XP/world travel/training or an end-to-end replay; that autonomous execution belongs to M0.6. ## M0.6 — One-run autonomous Decisions-style simulation and answer (owner-approved 2026-11-09)

**Product capability built progressively during the M0.4/M0.5 optimizer delivery, not a tenth M1 acceptance gate.** Without waiting for separate 144-gate certification, a user can request the same complete answer with **one high-level Forever/gsim simulation/optimization invocation**, analogous to the earlier Decisions experience. No one needs to manually launch L20/L30/L40/L60 simulations, separately kick off gear/talent/APL/pet searches, hand-select short-list winners, start 100-mob checks, trigger the dual-spec solver, assemble level frontiers, or stitch a final replay. **The software itself decides what to simulate next** from its observations and budget, calls the exact pinned native Rust combat/scenario executor, and returns the best known legal continuous strategy and its evidence. One invocation can orchestrate many real native calls; it does **not** mean a single giant Rust call, removal of measurement, or a fixed preselected winner.

**Required automatic end-to-end behavior:** given class=Warlock, start/end levels (10–60 initially), server/ruleset, scenario, constraints, source/build identity and compute budget, Forever/gsim discovers available options, constructs/caches comparable native-paid candidate evaluations, adaptively allocates its short-vs-100-mob evaluations, optimizes viable gear × talents × APL × pet × pull/recovery choices, and builds a **legal continuous level-by-level** progression. At L40, it jointly optimizes optional dual-spec acquisition/second talent path/active-spec switches with real sourced costs and an always-feasible single-spec comparator. Then **automatically executes a real native-backed end-to-end progression/replay** on the selected route (and necessary baseline/finalist validation), detects unsupported or failed candidates, and publishes machine-readable frontiers/decisions, reproducible receipts and a human-readable play guide showing talent points, spell ranks, gear, pet, rotations, pulls, regen, deaths, switching and measured/estimated leveling time.

**Acceptance of this capability:** from a clean, reproducible checkout with a compatible pinned Rust engine and available scenario data, **one documented CLI/API request** completes the orchestration, searches and real validations, then returns/publishes the requested Warlock route and dual-spec/single-spec comparison **without human-intervened intermediate triggers or manually supplied winner builds**. A targeted integration test must exercise that high-level interface, including internal invocation of actual native execution and recovery and an inspectable result (use smaller bounded levels/budgets for CI; full 10–60 requires a paid end-to-end demonstration). Report computation budget, actual evaluation counts, engine SHA, seed/scenario, coverage, unsupported mechanics and any modeled/provisional segments. Fail honestly rather than silently falling back to a Python combat simulation, an old cached answer, independent local level winners or a stitched fake full-route receipt. Reuse trustworthy compatible cache/results where valid; re-evaluate changed/missing finalists automatically. The initial Warlock implementation should use **generic orchestration interfaces reusable for all nine classes** without inventing class-specific game rules.

**Coordination of M0.5, M0.6 and M1:** M0.5 completes 20 → 10 → 5 → 1 density of the same native measured generic optimizer, starting at L20 if desired, without demanding a separate full XP/world/dual-spec replay. M0.6 extends that exact engine to autonomous continuous progression, training/travel and actual end-to-end replay. M1 remains a cumulative immutable ledger with its separately approved nine gates; neither the product optimizer nor M0.6 automatically grants new PASSes. M1 remains a cumulative, immutable ledger of the nine officially accepted gates per checkpoint. Neither product result nor orchestration grants new PASSes without the original approved recorder; no new implementation change ever reopens a recorded PASS.
