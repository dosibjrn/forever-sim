## Single Warcraft authority — explicit owner decision 2026-11-06

**MythicSim Rust is the single authority for Warcraft game data, item/equipment legality and the effects of candidate builds. The optimizer must submit selected gear/build/rotation candidates to the version-pinned Rust interface, let Rust evaluate them, and use the actual native result, refusal and measured objective. Never independently implement or re-check the same Warcraft rule in Forever.**

- **One-way boundary:** Forever proposes/searches builds, constructs scenarios, calls the pinned native interface and ranks/records real evaluated results. Rust owns item sources, class/level/race equipment proficiency, usable slots, talent/spell mechanics and actual combat evaluation. A native `VALID`/`INVALID`/`UNSUPPORTED` or executable refusal is authoritative. Do not replace it with a Python class whitelist, parallel item legality filter, duplicated formulas, substitute simulated combat, synthetic weights or a Python-derived permission/veto.
- **No false gates:** Source introspection, naked-character survival, a full source-catalogue proof, spell learnability completeness, actor/pet feature checklists or repeated Rust-validation certificates are **not independent prerequisites** for an existing P3–P7.5 PASS. The only acceptance predicates are the nine approved contracts. In particular **P3 tests genuinely scored native-equipped legal alternatives and meaningful measured score sensitivity, not a naked-kill prerequisite**. If the actual selected Rust API refuses a required candidate or cannot execute an actual selected checkpoint, preserve its precise refusal as execution evidence; do not relabel a historical proxy failure as a gate or force a new readiness gate.
- **Remove redundant code:** Clean, after live callers are safely migrated to the single native path, deletes duplicated Python GameIR, independent class/item/talent/ability legality rules, legacy fallback gameplay and artificial acceptance guards (see [#357](https://github.com/dosibjrn/forever-sim/issues/357) and [#359](https://github.com/dosibjrn/forever-sim/issues/359)). Delete dead tests with the dead implementation and retain focused integration regressions. This cleanup is code removal, **not** a second migration/validation gate, and must preserve real active acceptance and previously earned PASSes.

- Exact-SHA-pinned **MythicSim Rust** owns Warcraft facts, legal item/enchant/talent/APL choices and mechanics; approved Go execution remains possible. Forever owns scenario, generic gear × talents × policy proposals, paid candidate evaluation and selection. Consume real native VALID/INVALID/UNSUPPORTED; do not clone Warcraft rules in Python, revalidate MythicSim or revive GameIR.
- **P3:** finite source-legal gear scores and real score sensitivity to mechanically relevant changes, using a bounded representative legal item set. Mechanically irrelevant weights may be zero. No invented positive epsilon, source catalogue audit or global gear optimality claim.
- **P4/P5:** one real fresh **300s cold whole-build search**, capturing an immutable genuine **30s** best-so-far snapshot. Continue real paid proposals/evaluations beyond 30s; each chosen distinct winner needs its own real **100 consecutive-mob** retained-actor spatial world/native run including combat, movement, recovery, resources and survival. No independent second cold restart, representative unlock-cast certificate, blanket execution of every unlocked action or `N300 >= 2×N30` throughput gate. - **P6:** require comparable `T30 <= 1.01 × T300` seconds/mob on genuine full 100-mob measurements: the 30s snapshot must be within 1% of the 300s winner (the previously approved saturation criterion). Reuse identical genuine finalist receipts and replay only missing/changed/incomparable winners. No one-mob, fake or post-hoc substitute.
- **P7:** fixed source-legal **stationary non-attacking DPS** encounter; direct genuine pinned Rust/Go native evaluation is sufficient (no P7-only 2D world handshake). P7.1 measures one reproducible pinned **1,000-stochastic** reference with a 99% reference floor and may reuse an identical authentic native-generated receipt. P7.2/.3/.4 independently change the rotation/talent/gear axis, freeze other axes, and genuinely score at least one native-legal changed candidate when available; no separate 300s/1,000-stochastic/99% axis qualification. P7.5 alone runs an independent **300s cold joint gear × talents × policy search**, freezes its winner and holds out **1,000 stochastic native fights**, requiring mean DPS `>= 0.93 × max(reference, any accepted-compatible stochastic prior mean)`. Only actual stochastic prior means qualify as comparators; absent optional axis holdouts do not block P7.5.

## Native product search: one optimizer, sparse and dense are parameters (owner decisions 2026-11-10, 2026-11-11)

- **Public interface:** `optimize(start_level, end_level, level_stride, mob_count, search_budget, ...)` or equivalent one CLI request. `(20,60,20,2)` covers L20/L40/L60 for the **first real M0.4 demonstration**. M0.5 refines only `level_stride` 20 → 10 → 5 → 1 (start L20 allowed, 2 mobs valid); `(10,60,1,100)` is a later optional/full-resolution configuration using the identical optimizer, not a first deliverable or distinct pipeline. Do not implement special sparse/full pipelines or launch 5 independent per-level optimizations. The call orchestrates native candidate executions as needed; native engine capabilities may constrain legal values (current world chain supports 2–100 mobs).
- **Every evaluation pays an end condition:** After `mob_count` genuine hostile native mob kills, run executable **full HP and full mana recovery**, charge all extra elapsed time/resources and include it in the candidate's performance score. Choose feasible native Life Tap/eat/drink/passive recovery; Life Tap changes *both* HP and mana and may require eating afterward. Neither free state refill nor a fixed arbitrary number of recovery seconds is acceptable. If full restoration cannot be completed/observed, keep the genuine partial/refusal and do not fabricate a comparable recovered score. This sample boundary normalizes resource debt; it is a search estimate, not a claim of free resource resets during an actual route.
- **Deterministic search:** Use pinned Rust `rng_mode=balanced` and **one iteration per scored candidate** by default. The deterministic cumulative quota RNG is reproducible and carries accounting over events/fights; multiple balanced repetitions are not independent stochastic repeats. One 3-mob sample does not prove rare proc or long-run rankings. Use 5–10 mobs, larger target counts or stochastic confirmation *only* when comparisons or accepted unchanged M1 contracts justify them.
- **Performance selection:** Generate multiple source-native legal talent/path builds across trees and hybrids plus gear, pet, APL, pulling and paid recovery; use actual scored comparisons, retain more than one viable legal path at a sampled level, and advance by cumulative paid/estimated elapsed combat-and-recovery score along compatible sampled-level talent decisions. `options → prepare → first legal candidate → 100 mobs` and talent-index greedy fills remain **baselines**. No 100-mob paid continuation merely to grow a first-legal coverage counter. Optional dual-spec/respec costs belong in the scorer only when sourced and available; do not create XP/travel/training or retained actor prerequisites.
- **Honest outputs:** Publish sampled levels, stride, mob count, iteration count, compared alternatives, actual full terminal resource state, charged terminal recovery time, engine identity, elapsed seconds, reuse and budget. List fixed/unsupported dimensions and scope best-of-sampled winners. Legal compatibility across sampled level snapshots must be sourced; unmeasured levels must not be described as simulated. Compatible old paid baselines are immutable and reusable; no new M1 gate, accepted PASS replay or source-native rules clone.
