# P3 — native-backed gear-scoring smoke

Apply [COMMON](COMMON.md). This is a **bounded scorer/gear integration test**, not a best-in-slot search or a full item-catalogue certification.

1. Ask the selected genuine source/native authority for equipment legally available to the checkpoint build. For each **occupied slot**, score a representative **one or two source-legal item alternatives** when available (or the single legal option); record items, legality and real chosen build/context. If an occupied slot has no confirmed legal option, report the actual refusal instead of inventing an item.
2. The generic production gear scorer returns **finite, reproducible scores** for real source-legal alternatives. Demonstrate paid score sensitivity to at least one **mechanically relevant native-executable** stat/item change when such a change is available. Expose relevant unsupported changes honestly; they cannot be silently counted as passed.
3. A stat is not required to have a strictly positive weight merely because an item exposes it. **Mechanically irrelevant stats may have zero weight**; a genuine negative trade-off may be reported rather than clipped. No fake epsilon, hard-coded class weight, unrelated weapon-damage derivative or duplicate Python rules authority. Mechanical applicability follows the real selected actor, gear, rotation and native effects, not a class whitelist.

Keep inspectable item IDs, full evaluated contexts, native/scorer identity and genuine measured differences. No top-five-by-ilvl in every slot, mandatory positives for Spirit/Agility/MP5/Crit/Hit/etc. regardless of relevance, exhaustive source catalogue audit, effect-completeness proof or 100-mob marginal perturbation is a P3 prerequisite.
