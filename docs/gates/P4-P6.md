# P4–P6 — real whole-build leveling optimization

Apply [COMMON](COMMON.md). Optimize **source-legal gear × talents × policy × pull/recovery/survival** on one pinned 100-mob objective, in the actual selected consistent spatial world/native runtime. Preserve one persistent actor's health, mana/resources, cooldowns, buffs, movement/CC, pets, survival and paid regeneration between **100 consecutive distinct mobs**. A single-target smoke, simulation proxy, reset-per-kill actor or post-hoc stitched clocks cannot PASS.

## P4 — real cold 30-second snapshot

Start one fresh, unseeded, genuinely paid **300-second** whole-build search. Freeze the actual best legal source-paid candidate and objective at **30 elapsed wall seconds** (configuration, engine/source/scenario, timestamp and scored candidate receipt). P4 requires that snapshot's selected winner to have a genuine completed persistent-actor 100-mob result; it may be evaluated during or after the shared search. Do not seed a saved legacy/reference winner.

Native-sourced generation must be able to propose **any** reachable talent-unlocked action. Where at least one reachable unlock exists, execute and compare a legal policy actually casting at least **one representative unlocked action**, with real cast/result evidence. No mandatory exhaustive real execution of **every** unlocked action during 30 seconds; an unused action alone is not a FAIL.

## P5 — real 300-second continuation

Continue **the same genuine search** to the **300-second wall-clock** boundary and freeze its final best source-paid complete legal candidate. Real proposal/evaluation effort must continue after 30 seconds, with at least **two distinct paid full builds across the run when legal alternatives exist**. Report the true unique evaluated count and full winning build. P5 requires a real completed persistent 100-mob winner result. No arbitrary `N300 >= 2 × N30` count ratio or independent second cold start.

## P6 — measured saturation, no duplicate execution

Compare P4's and P5's genuine winner routes on the same source, ruleset, engine, target sequence, scenario, starting actor state and measurement semantics. Require **`T30 <= 1.01 × T300`** for comparable mean seconds per mob. Reuse an **already genuinely executed identical** 100-mob receipt when build, scenario, engine, initial state, seed/statistical protocol and runtime behavior match; execute only missing, changed or incomparable winner runs. This is evidence reuse, not a waiver of a distinct build's actual 100 kills, travel, paid recovery or survival.
